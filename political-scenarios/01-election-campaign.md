# Scenario: The Valoria General Election (fictional)

**Prediction question to ask MiroFish:** "How will public opinion and online discussion shift over the final four weeks of the campaign, and which party is likely to win the most seats?"

## Setting
Valoria is a mid-sized parliamentary democracy of 24 million people. A general election is held in four weeks. Parliament has 300 seats; 151 are needed for a majority. Turnout at the last election was 68%.

## Parties
- **Civic Progress Party (CPP)** – centre-left, incumbent. Leader: Prime Minister Elena Marsh, 54, former economist. Polling at 31%. Platform: expand public healthcare, raise the minimum wage, carbon tax on heavy industry.
- **National Renewal (NR)** – centre-right, main opposition. Leader: Tomas Kerrigan, 47, former regional governor. Polling at 33%. Platform: income tax cuts, stricter immigration quotas, deregulation for small businesses.
- **Green Future (GF)** – environmentalist, polling at 11%. Leader: Amira Osei, 39. Kingmaker in coalition talks; leans toward CPP but opposes its slow coal phase-out.
- **Valoria First (VF)** – populist, anti-establishment, polling at 14% and rising. Leader: Dax Holloway, 51, former TV host. Runs a heavy social media campaign attacking "elites in the capital".
- **Undecided voters:** about 11%, concentrated among under-30s and rural voters.

## Key issues
1. **Cost of living:** inflation is 6.2%; rents in the capital rose 18% in two years.
2. **Immigration:** 120,000 arrivals last year after a regional conflict; northern towns report strained housing.
3. **Climate:** a record summer drought hurt farmers; GF and CPP push climate action, NR and VF frame it as a cost burden.
4. **Corruption scandal:** two weeks ago a newspaper reported that a CPP cabinet minister steered a $40M rail contract to a donor's firm. The minister resigned; Marsh denies knowledge.

## Media and online landscape
- *The Valoria Herald* (centre-left newspaper) broke the rail story.
- *Capital Daily* (centre-right tabloid) runs daily anti-CPP headlines.
- Influencers: "@RuralVoiceVal" (farmers' advocate, 400k followers), "@GenZValoria" (youth politics account, 650k followers), economist Prof. Lin Bao (respected, neutral).
- VF's posts get the highest engagement; fact-checkers have flagged several as misleading.

## Upcoming events (variables you can inject)
- Week 2: televised leaders' debate.
- Week 3: central bank interest rate decision.
- Week 4: final polls; possible further leaks about the rail contract.
