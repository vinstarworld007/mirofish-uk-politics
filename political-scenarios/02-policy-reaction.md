# Scenario: Public reaction to the Fuel Subsidy Reform Bill (fictional)

**Prediction question to ask MiroFish:** "How will different groups react online to the fuel subsidy removal over the next month, will protests grow or fade, and is the government likely to back down?"

## Background
The Republic of Meridia (population 60 million) has subsidised petrol and diesel for 20 years, costing 3.5% of GDP per year. Facing an IMF-backed budget plan, President Sofia Arden's government announced the **Fuel Subsidy Reform Bill**:
- Fuel subsidies end in three stages over 9 months; pump prices rise roughly 45% in total.
- Savings fund a monthly cash transfer of $25 to the poorest 40% of households, plus free school meals.
- Public transport fares are frozen for one year.

## Stakeholders
- **Government:** President Arden (reformist, second term, 52% approval before the announcement); Finance Minister Raj Patel, the bill's architect.
- **Opposition:** People's Alliance, led by Marcus Obi, calls the bill "an attack on working families" and demands a referendum.
- **Unions:** Transport Workers Federation (1.2M members) threatens a national strike; teachers' union supports the school meals part.
- **Business:** the Chamber of Commerce supports the reform for fiscal stability; trucking and taxi associations strongly oppose it.
- **Citizens:** urban commuters, rural farmers who depend on diesel, students, low-income families who would get the cash transfer, middle-class car owners who get nothing.
- **Economists:** most agree the subsidy mostly benefits wealthier households, but warn about short-term inflation.
- **Media:** state broadcaster MBC (pro-government), independent outlet *Meridia Now*, and widely shared viral videos of long queues at petrol stations.

## Current situation (day 0)
- Small protests in three cities; the hashtag #NoToFuelHike is trending.
- A government ad campaign explains the cash transfers, but registration for them opens only in 3 weeks.
- Opposition MPs need 20 more votes to block the bill; 15 ruling-party MPs from rural districts are wavering.

## Variables you can inject
- The transport union announces a strike date.
- The government delays stage 2 by three months.
- Global oil prices drop 15%.
- A protester is injured during clashes with police.
