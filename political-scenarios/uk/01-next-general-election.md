# UK scenario 1: The road to the next general election

Upload this together with `00-uk-politics-2015-2026.md`.

**Prediction question to paste into MiroFish:**
"Starting from October 2026, with Andy Burnham as the new Labour Prime Minister, how will public opinion and online debate in the UK evolve over the next year? Can Labour hold its recovery, which of the Conservatives, Reform UK or Restore Britain becomes the main right-wing challenger, and who is most likely to win the most seats at the next general election?"

## Simulation focus
- The Burnham "honeymoon": whether voters who left Labour in 2025–26 (to Reform, the Greens or abstention) return.
- The split on the right: Conservative recovery under Badenoch versus Reform's scandals and Restore Britain's insurgency, and whether any pact or merger pressure builds despite Badenoch ruling it out.
- The legacy of Brexit voter groups: how 2016 Leave voters in Red Wall towns and 2016 Remain voters in cities and the Blue Wall split their votes.
- The nations: whether the SNP and Plaid Cymru successes push independence and devolution up the agenda.

## Variables you can inject in later runs (one at a time)
- Burnham's first budget raises taxes on high earners and wealth to fund the NHS.
- Police charge a senior Reform UK figure over illegal donations.
- Farage steps down as Reform leader.
- A Conservative–Reform local election pact is announced despite Badenoch's denials.
- Small-boat crossings hit a record month.
- The UK and EU agree a youth mobility scheme and closer single-market alignment for goods.
